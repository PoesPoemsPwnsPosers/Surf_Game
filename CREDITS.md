## Art, sound and music

No third-party assets are used yet.

Everything on screen so far (surfer, board, ocean, palms, buildings, buoys, clouds, the pier,
the surf shop, benches and lifeguard towers) is built from simple shapes in code written for
this project. The shop sign is drawn with the browser's own fonts. These are placeholders.

When asset packs are added (planned for milestone 5), each one will be listed here with
its author, source link and license. CC0 packs such as Kenney and Quaternius are preferred.
